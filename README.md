## My Philosophy: Turning Obsession Into Defense

I don't have a background filled with expensive certifications or a massive corporate tech history. What I do have is a **deep obsession with figuring out how things break and how to defend them**. 

For me, cybersecurity isn't a passive career choice; it's a daily habit. The threat landscape changes every single hour, and I genuinely love waking up knowing there is a new puzzle to solve, a new log source to understand, or a new attack vector to analyze.

## ⚡ The Pivot: BPO to Cyber Defense

I am actively transitioning from the **BPO industry** into cybersecurity while pursuing my **Bachelor’s Degree in IT (Networking & Cybersecurity)**. My time in BPO wasn't a detour—it was a training ground. It taught me how to communicate under high pressure, think critically on the fly, and maintain flawless documentation. I am bringing that exact battle-tested energy straight to a **Tier 1 SOC team**.

Right now, my days are split between my university curriculum, hunting flags on **TryHackMe**, studying for my **CompTIA Security+**, and documenting my journey.
